I finally decided to create a space to write. After more than 17 years in tech (the last 7 focused on DevOps/SRE), I have accumulated a lot worth documenting: experiments that worked (and the ones that didn't), architecture decisions, automation, and lessons I wish I had read years ago.

## What you will find here

My day-to-day focus is **SRE and platform engineering**, so most posts will revolve around:

- **Kubernetes and GitOps**: ArgoCD, rollout strategies, cluster management, and those weird issues that only show up in production;
- **IaC**: Terraform, Terragrunt, Packer and Ansible, including patterns for structuring infrastructure that scales;
- **Observability**: Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, SLOs, and how to stop staring at dashboards;
- **Incidents and post-mortems**: incident response culture, war rooms, and real lessons learned;
- **FinOps**: reducing cloud costs without breaking anything that works;
- **Applied AI for platform engineering**: agents, RAG, and LLMs integrated with SRE tooling.

## Why write?

Three simple reasons:

1. **Writing helps thinking**: documenting a decision clearly exposes gaps before they become incidents.
2. **Giving back to the community**: I learned a lot from blogs and open source; it is time to return the favor.
3. **A record of the journey**: tomorrow I will forget the details of today.
